# Rocket

Each rocket is constructed in a manufacturing line at a [building](gameplay/concepts/building.md) from a template. 

Once built, a rocket can be moved around in a [site](gameplay/concepts/site.md) to be stored in a larger warehouse or automatically delivered to a launchpad as part of a [launch plan](gameplay/concepts/launch-plan.md).

Rockets have various **lift capacities** for sending [payloads](gameplay/concepts/contract.md) to orbit. The lift capacity determines *how much* mass the rocket can send to that particular orbit and reduces the higher or more eccentric the orbit is.
