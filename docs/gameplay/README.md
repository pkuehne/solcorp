# Gameplay

SolCorp is a space launch company management simulation. You build and operate launch facilities, hire and manage staff, manufacture rockets, and fulfil contracts from clients.

## Contents

- [Core Concepts](core-concepts.md) — Sites, buildings, facilities, staff
- [Contracts & Launches](contracts-and-launches.md) — The contract pipeline and launch operations
- [Simulation](simulation.md) — Orbital mechanics and celestial bodies

## The Game Loop

1. **Build** — Construct buildings at your launch site to unlock capabilities (launchpads, offices, storage, factories)
2. **Staff** — Hire personnel to operate facilities and prepare launches
3. **Manufacture** — Produce rockets in your factory
4. **Contract** — Accept contracts from clients specifying payload, orbit, and reward
5. **Launch** — Assign a rocket to a contract and execute the launch
6. **Repeat** — Use revenue to expand your site and take on more ambitious contracts
