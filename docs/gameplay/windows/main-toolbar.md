# Toolbar

## Simulation Controls

The first item in the toolbar controls the simulation itself. There is a play/pause button to allow the simulation to run as well as an indicator of what the current day is.

## Company Balance

Next to the play/pause button is the name of your corporation and your current balance, which is the amount of money you have available to spend on building, staffing, manufacturing, and fulfilling contracts. Your balance will increase when you successfully complete contracts and decrease when you spend money on operations.

## Windows

Below the company information is the Window Toolbar, which allows you to open various UI windows to manage different aspects of your site and operations. These include:

- **Celestial Browser** — View information about celestial bodies and orbits
- **Launch Planner** — Plan and execute rocket launches
- **Active Launches** — Monitor the status of ongoing launches
- **Rockets** — View and manage your rocket inventory
- **Contracts** — View and manage available contracts

## Weather

Weather conditions can affect launch operations. The current weather is displayed in the top right corner of the screen. Weather conditions range from sunny to thunderstorms, and each condition can have different effects on launch success rates and preparation times. For example, thunderstorms may increase the failure rate of launches.

## Notifications

A lot of things happen in Solcorp automatically or behind the scenes. You will receive notifications for important events such as contract issuance, launch completions, and manufacturing progress. Notifications appear in the top right corner of the screen and can be clicked to view more details about the event.
